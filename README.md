# 📊 Amazon Sales Analysis – Power BI Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Amazon product sales across categories, time periods and customer reviews.
## 🎯 Objective
Track sales performance, identify top products and categories, and understand monthly and weekly trends.
## 📌 Key Metrics
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| YTD Sales | $2.18M |
| QTD Sales | $811.09K |
| YTD Products Sold | 27.75K |
| YTD Reviews | 19.42M |
## 🔍 Key Insights
- **Men Shoes** leads with $940K (43.18% of sales), followed by **Camera** at $492K (22.62%) and **Men Clothes** at 16.42%.
- Sales jump in **September**, dip in **October**, then peak in **November–December** (about $1.3M per month).
- Weekly sales are strongest in the last quarter (weeks 36–52).
- The top product by sales is a **Nikon** product ($34K). The top by reviews is a **SanDisk** product (0.40M).
## 📈 Dashboard Features
- KPI cards: YTD Sales, QTD Sales, Products Sold, Reviews
- Sales by Month (area chart) and Sales by Week (bar chart)
- Sales by Product Category table with % contribution
- Top 5 Products by Sales and by Reviews
- Filters: Price, Quarter, Product Category
## 🛠️ Tools Used
Power BI Desktop · DAX · Power Query · Excel/CSV
